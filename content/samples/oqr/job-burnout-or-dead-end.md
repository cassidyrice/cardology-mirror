## 1. Birth card

You are a 6 of Clubs. The number is the action: a six is responsibility and recalibration. The suit is the area: clubs are ideas, language, and the stories a room uses to see the work. The working range passes on what you know without disappearing behind the lesson. The slip stays in the teacher seat after learning has stopped, or gives the knowledge away and keeps your own authority quiet. The role feels heavier than the title because you are carrying the knowing for the room.

## 2. Long Range

This year’s Long Range card is the 8 of Spades. An eight is power and mastery; spades are work, will, and transformation. It keeps pulling attention toward the hard lead, and whether you stay after the call is made. The working range takes that lead and remains. The slip wins the room by force of mind until people stop bringing you anything they cannot defend. Unused, the same power steps off the hook and resents whoever picked it up.

## 3. Pluto and its payoff

This year’s Pluto card is the Jack of Spades. A jack experiments, in the suit of the work itself. The seat names what the year is working: say what is actually happening, and remain after you have said it. The slip says the hard thing on the way out. The fact lands. The repair does not, because you are already gone.

The payoff is the 3 of Hearts. A three is choice; hearts are emotional truth. The working range lets something real have range without using newness as the exit. The slip keeps a spare door open, or finds a fatal flaw the moment staying is required. Name the work. A new option is not the only way out of a feeling. Neither card answers quit or stay.

## 4. Card you owe

The card you owe this year is the 7 of Clubs. A seven tests; clubs are the mind. This is how the question tends to go wrong: you can name every flaw, so the plan never leaves the table. Analysis does the job of a decision. Another week of research protects you from finding out whether the role is finished or you are tired.

## 5. Card you’re owed

The card you’re owed this year is the 7 of Hearts. Same number, in feeling rather than argument. What works without trying is looking clearly without using analysis as a way not to feel it. The slip understands the feeling instead of having it, and calls the distance discernment. Let the felt weight of the job sit beside the case you have built. That is a pattern of attention, not a health claim.

## 6. The question

Dead end, or burned out: the cards do not pick. The 6 of Clubs carries other people’s knowing until any role feels like yours to hold. The 8 of Spades either runs the room until it goes quiet, or steps off and resents the quiet. The Jack of Spades asks you to say what is happening and still be there. The 7 of Clubs can make a tired season look permanent, because the decision never ships. The 7 of Hearts is where “I am depleted” and “this is over” stop being one sentence. The mirror shows the coat. You decide which one is in it.

## 7. Three things to watch

Over the next few weeks, three patterns are worth catching. They are attention points, not a calendar of events.

You explain something to someone who did not ask. That is the 6 of Clubs back in the teacher seat.

You win the meeting, then notice how long it takes before bad news comes back. That is the 8 of Spades emptying the room.

You research another week instead of a rough version of the decision: stay, change the work, or leave. That is the 7 of Clubs using thoroughness as a closed door.
