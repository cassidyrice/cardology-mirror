## 1. Birth card

You are a 9 of Diamonds. The number is the action: a nine completes and releases. The suit is the area: diamonds are values, resources, and what you count as worth. The working range gives in a way you can sustain, and lets someone give back. The slip funds the other person’s floor and skips your own. Receiving starts to feel like a failure of character, so you walk in already tired.

## 2. Long Range

This year’s Long Range card is the 2 of Spades. A two is partnership and exchange; spades are work and transformation. It is what keeps pulling attention: two pressures held at once. The working range holds both and still chooses. The slip sees both sides so clearly that fairness becomes a way not to stand anywhere. The year keeps returning you to the choice you leave open.

## 3. Pluto and its payoff

This year’s Pluto card is the Ace of Hearts. An ace initiates; hearts are emotional truth and the courage to stay open. The seat names what the year is working: say what you feel, and leave room for an answer. The slip reaches so hard that the other person has to manage the reach.

The payoff is the 4 of Spades. A four is foundation, in the suit of work and will. Its working range builds enough container that a change can happen without the floor disappearing. Beside the Ace, that is an opening and a structure that can hold it. The same four can brace against change and call the brace stability.

## 4. Card you owe

The card you owe this year is the King of Hearts: command, in the feeling of the room. This is how the question tends to go wrong. You lead the feeling from a distance you will not cross. People can lean on the authority and cannot reach you. Or you decide the climate, they comply, and they stop telling you the truth. The move is the throne, so you never have to be met.

## 5. Card you’re owed

The card you’re owed this year is the 10 of Hearts: belonging at full scale. What works without trying is making room for other people and still keeping a self in the room. The slip holds the group’s feeling until you cannot feel your own, and being needed stands in for being met. It is the move this question usually skips.

## 6. The question

The same kind of relationship is this stack, already running. The 9 of Diamonds gives past empty and calls it devotion. The 2 of Spades keeps both versions alive so you never stand on one. The Ace of Hearts reaches before there is a place for the feeling, and the King of Hearts runs the climate from too far away for anyone to correct you. That does not name who shows up, or what they choose. The 10 of Hearts is the other seat: room for them, and a self that is still yours. The mirror leaves the next move with you.

## 7. Three things to watch

Over the next few weeks, three patterns are worth catching. They are attention points, not a calendar of events.

The gift you give while tired, and the resentment that follows. That is the 9 of Diamonds, then the bill.

The decision you leave open until someone else makes it. That is the 2 of Spades calling indecision fairness.

The next time you say the feeling first, count whether you leave a silence for their answer. If the room agrees too fast, notice what nobody said. That is the Ace reaching, and the King setting the climate.
