# Period library style guide

Version: 2026-09-30.library-completion.v1. This brief applies to the static period and yearly reading library, chapter copy, notifications, onboarding, and share captions. The editorial contract governs record shape and word lengths.

## Write a recognizable scene

Use close second person and present tense. Begin with a particular tension the card helps the reader examine: a message sent twice, an unasked favor, a decision left open, a detail held back, or a standard raised after it is met. Examples are possibilities, not claims about a person's biography. Leave room for the passage not to fit.

Explain the behavior, then its practical cost. A shadow is something a person does, not a defect in who they are. Show the useful form beside the habit that makes it difficult. Give a small action with a clear stopping point. Ask a real question about a choice rather than instructing the reader to agree with your conclusion.

Write the actual card first. Use the current bible's sweet spot, shadow, cost, and watch-for detail as the meaning anchor. Let the position or planet alter what is being examined. Do not substitute a generic rank essay for the card's specific behavior. Do not write one paragraph and swap card, suit, planet, or role labels to populate the matrix.

Keep sentences plain and connected. Prefer a concrete verb to an abstract explanation. Use a short sentence where the point needs weight, but do not force every sentence into the same rhythm. Vary the opening, scene, paragraph shape, practical action, and question across adjacent records.

## Apply the correct lens

| Lens | What the writing examines |
| --- | --- |
| Mercury | Words, questions, messages, and decisions |
| Venus | Desire, value, care, and reciprocity |
| Mars | Action, boundaries, and response to pressure |
| Jupiter | Growth, scale, generosity, and proportion |
| Saturn | Structure, limits, responsibility, and follow-through |
| Uranus | Freedom, experiments, and useful revision |
| Neptune | Imagination, uncertainty, release, and a grounded anchor |

A planet colors the reading. It does not establish an event, its cause, or its date. Keep Spades grounded in work, effort, ordinary limits, habits, and responsibility. Do not turn the body into a warning system or add diagnosis, treatment, illness, death, pregnancy, or breakup predictions. Diamonds can examine resources, exchange, and worth without returns, financial guarantees, or buy/sell advice.

For ranks, preserve the contract's emphasis: Ace begins; Two partners and chooses; Three creates and weighs alternatives; Four stabilizes; Five changes; Six repeats responsibility and reciprocity; Seven tests a claim; Eight handles mastery and control; Nine completes and releases; Ten holds greater capacity and groups. Jack experiments, Queen stewards, and King carries authority. The actual bible supplies the more specific meaning within each rank and suit.

Threshold, karmic, and structural are editorial significance labels. They do not prove a stronger event or a deserved consequence. Aces, Sixes, and Nines retain the deeper period-reading treatment specified in the contract. The correct weighted-rank count is 84, not the August report's 156.

## Keep yearly positions distinct

| Role | Useful question for the writer |
| --- | --- |
| Long Range | What deserves repeated attention across the selected birthday year? |
| Pluto | What desired growth puts pressure on a recognizable habit? |
| Result | What possible capacity or resource could be useful beside that work? |
| Environment | How might the actual assigned card's useful side support the present task? |
| Displacement | What responsibility or growth edge becomes visible from the actual assigned seat? |

Use conditional resource framing for Result. Then describe how the capacity works in a concrete choice. Keep outcome claims out of the paragraph without adding a repeated list of things the card does not promise. The integrity shell carries the general explanation. Long Range does not make every moment about one theme. Pluto does not declare an unavoidable crisis. Environment does not supply a person, skill, or material resource absent from the reader's actual circumstances. Displacement does not turn every difficulty into required work.

Court figures describe roles a person of any age or gender can hold. Show the role through behavior, such as a guide who shares a needed fact or an authority who hears a difficult reply. A role may be the reader's posture or a way someone already present behaves. Do not forecast a man, woman, younger person, admirer, or mentor arriving.

Yearly records contain one individually authored reading of 65 to 110 words, plus a separate reflection question. The delivered readings are 74 to 101 words. Their availability in a 52-card lookup library is not evidence that every position is assigned to every user.

## Write connective copy only when the relation is real

Use a connector to clarify the actual choice after the period reading. Select at most one or two. Return from the yearly lens to a small observable scene. A useful connector names why the two views help the reader think, rather than adding a second summary of every card.

Use the 18 reviewed connectors in supporting-copy.json when their required values and relations are present. Replace a placeholder only with a displayed value from the selected calculated packet. Never expose an unresolved placeholder. Skip a connector whose required assignment is absent.

Environment and Displacement copy renders only for actual non-null cards supplied for those yearly roles. Never fill a missing yearly role from lifetime karma, another year, or a library row. Fixed cards are Eight of Clubs, Jack of Hearts, and King of Spades. Their own quiet, useful, and excessive forms remain available without inventing an owe-and-owed pair.

Semi-fixed lifetime twins are Ace of Clubs with Two of Hearts, and Seven of Diamonds with Nine of Hearts. For lifetime karma, the owed and owing card is the same twin. This explanation does not determine the selected year's Environment or Displacement card. The engine packet determines yearly eligibility. The August report's blanket exclusion of all seven special cards is not a valid replacement for that packet.

Use an echo connector only when the calculated packet explicitly marks ECHO and identifies the exact relationship. Use an exact repeated-card connector only after verifying equal card codes and the displayed yearly role. A shared rank, suit, or broad theme creates neither an echo nor a Pluto activation window. Do not invent a timing window from a semantic resemblance.

## Dates, notifications, and sharing

Use runtime start and end labels from the same selected birthday-year chapter. Do not copy the illustrative August sample dates, invent a calendar date, or require an answer by day 52. Neptune may be longer than the other chapters.

The notification library has seven planets, four moments, and two tones. before14 and before1 are scheduled relative to the actual chapter start. opening uses the actual start. midpoint uses the actual start and end with the app's calendar conventions; it is not automatically day 26 for Neptune. The only notification placeholders are startLabel and endLabel. Suppress date-bearing copy when those dates are unavailable or invalid. The library supplies words; it does not establish that notifications are enabled, scheduled, or delivered.

Plain notification copy names the timing and one usable point of attention. Warm copy offers room for an ordinary choice. Keep lock-screen text free of health hints, fear, presumed relationship status, promised gains, and claims of impending crisis. Do not imply that reading or unlocking content prevents harm.

Share captions use only the chosen reading's displayed values. Review the caption before sharing. A birthdate, private question, or personal note belongs in a caption only if the user chooses to include it. If dates are unavailable, use the reviewed date-free subtitle. Do not expose empty variables or describe a planned sharing feature as already delivered.

## Final editorial pass

Check each record for the correct card, role or planet, source references, and length. Read it aloud for recognizable behavior, a clear cost, and a choice the reader still owns. Compare adjacent entries for copied scenes, repeated opening rhythms, and actions that differ only by nouns. An exact-duplicate scan is useful, but unique strings alone do not establish distinct writing.

Exclude em dashes, exclamation points, markdown inside record strings, and the contract's banned certainty or mystic vocabulary: you will, expect, fate, destiny, manifestation, universe, vibration, sacred, divine, and journey. Avoid verdicts, mockery, diagnoses, coercion, and repeated disclaimers inside readings. A hard passage ends with an available choice rather than doom.

For period records, preserve the contract's long-reading word bands: 400 to 600 for A, 6, and 9; 250 to 350 for 2, 4, and 10; 150 to 250 for the remaining ranks. Keep the required fields and meaningful court person-or-posture sentence. Do not apply the newer one-question product's 500-to-700-word layout to this library.
